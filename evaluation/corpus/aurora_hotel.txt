Aurora Hotel — family stay

Aurora Hotel offers free cancellation until 48 hours before arrival. Later cancellations cost the first night's room rate.
Children under 6 stay free when using existing beds. A family room accommodates up to 4 people.
Breakfast is included and served from 07:00 to 10:00.
An airport shuttle costs EUR 12 per person each way and must be booked one day in advance.
Two accessible rooms can be requested. The hotel has no swimming pool.
Check-in starts at 14:00; checkout is by 11:00.
