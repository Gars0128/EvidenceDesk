Shared booking terms

Each activity's own cancellation deadline applies. Inside its free cancellation window, cancellation returns the full ticket price.
After that deadline, the cancellation fee is 100% of the ticket price unless a specific activity states an exception.
If the operator cancels an activity because of unsafe weather, all affected customers receive a full refund.
Groups of 6 or more people must request a group reservation in advance.
All departure times and cancellation deadlines use the destination's local time.
Hotel cancellation policies are governed by the individual hotel's conditions, not these activity terms.
