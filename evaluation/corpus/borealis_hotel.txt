Borealis Hotel — reservation conditions

All Borealis Hotel reservations are non-refundable. Cancelling a reservation does not return the room payment.
Guests must be at least 12 years old. Younger children cannot stay at this hotel.
The indoor swimming pool is open from 08:00 to 20:00.
Breakfast is not included in the room price and costs EUR 14 per person.
The airport shuttle is free for registered guests.
Check-in starts at 15:00; checkout is by 11:00.
