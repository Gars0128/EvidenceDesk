Castle Adventure — excursion program

Castle Adventure accepts children aged 8 and older. The route includes steep stairs, and strollers are not permitted.
The tour lasts 3 hours. Departure is every Saturday at 09:00 from Castle Square.
All Castle Adventure tickets are non-refundable, including customer cancellation before departure.
The price is EUR 35 per adult and EUR 20 per child aged 8 to 15.
The program includes the upper tower, the armory, and the underground passage.
Visitors must be able to climb stairs without assistance.
