Forest Cycling Trail — excursion program

The Forest Cycling Trail accepts participants aged 12 and older with a minimum height of 145 cm.
Helmets and bicycles are included. The guided ride lasts 4 hours.
Customer cancellations are non-refundable; there is no free customer cancellation window.
If the operator cancels because of unsafe weather, the customer receives a full refund.
Departure is every Sunday at 08:30 from Forest Gate.
The price is EUR 45 per participant. Riders must already know how to cycle.
