Harbor Discovery Walk — excursion program

The Harbor Discovery Walk accepts children aged 4 and older. Strollers are allowed on the entire route.
The guided walk lasts 90 minutes. It departs daily at 10:00 from Pier 2.
Free cancellation is available until 24 hours before departure. Later cancellation is non-refundable.
The price is EUR 18 per adult and EUR 8 per child aged 4 to 12.
The walk includes the fish market, the old lighthouse, and the waterfront promenade.
The route is mostly flat but includes one short cobbled section.
