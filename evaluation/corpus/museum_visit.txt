City Museum Visit — ticket information

The City Museum welcomes visitors of all ages. Children under 7 enter free.
Adult tickets cost EUR 15. Tickets for children aged 7 to 15 cost EUR 7.
The museum is wheelchair accessible and has lockers for bags. It is closed on Mondays.
Tickets can be cancelled for a full refund until 24 hours before the reserved entry time.
A typical visit takes 2 hours. On open days, entry is available between 09:00 and 17:00.
The permanent exhibition covers local history, maritime trade, and everyday life.
