River Lights Cruise — excursion program

The River Lights Cruise welcomes children of all ages. Infants under 3 travel free.
Child-sized life jackets are provided. The boat is wheelchair accessible.
The cruise lasts 60 minutes and departs daily at 14:00 from Pier 4.
Free cancellation is available until 12 hours before departure. Later customer cancellations are non-refundable.
The price is EUR 24 per adult and EUR 12 per child aged 3 to 12.
The cruise passes the historic bridge, the riverside gardens, and the old dockyard.
